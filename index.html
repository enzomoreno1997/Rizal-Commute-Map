Taxi and TNVS are meter estimates (₱65 flag-down for 500 m, then per-km and per-minute, assuming traffic is 1.5× free-flow). TNVS surge and pick-up fees are not included. Jeepneys and UV Express do not run every road, so use the figure as a ceiling for the distance.

Other fares

  • EDSA Busway (Carousel): station-to-station, ₱18 minimum and ₱85 from Monumento to PITX.
  • Tricycles: fares are set by each town or city, not by the LTFRB.

How fares are computed. Fare = minimum fare for the first band, plus the per-km rate for each km after it. Jeepneys use a 4 km band; city buses use 5 km. UV Express has no minimum, so it is rate × distance. The result is rounded to the nearest ₱0.25. The discount is 20% of the scale, rounded separately.

Discounts. Students, senior citizens and PWDs get 20% off. Carry your ID, because it is not applied automatically.

Provisional rates. UV Express and taxi increases were approved as provisional while petitions are decided. Fares can change, and the fare board in the vehicle is what counts.

Overcharged? Report it to the LTFRB hotline, 1342, with the plate or body number, route and time.

Map accuracy. Hub and station positions are approximate,. Road routes come from the free OpenStreetMap-based routing service (routing.openstreetmap.de), with backup servers. If all are unreachable, no line is drawn and the fares shown are estimates.
